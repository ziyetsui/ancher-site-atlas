The Studio

I run a two-colour riso studio and pull every print by hand.

Ten years in, the imperfection is the point — a pink that doesn't quite meet the blue, a halftone that breathes, edges that show the press did the work. I draw it, separate the colours, and run the drum until it sings. The misregister is mine; nobody else's will land the same.

House Rules

  1. 01

    Off-register on purpose

    The ghost edge where pink misses blue is the signature, not a defect. I lean into it.

  2. 02

    Pull it by hand

    Every sheet goes through the drum myself. No outsourcing the part that makes it mine.

  3. 03

    Spot colour, never CMYK

    Fluoro pink and process blue do what four-colour can't. I design within the inks I own.

  4. 04

    Paper does half the work

    Warm off-white stock, visible grain. The substrate is part of the picture, not a backdrop.
