05 principles — how I build

  1. 01

    Correct before fast

    A wrong answer returned quickly is still wrong. Nail the invariants first, then optimize the hot path with numbers, not vibes.

  2. 02

    Measure the tail, not the mean

    Averages hide the outages. I design for p99 and the failure modes nobody documents, because that's where users actually feel pain.

  3. 03

    Boring is a feature

    Reliable systems are mostly unremarkable. I'd rather ship something a teammate can reason about at 3am than something clever.

  4. 04

    Write it down

    If a design decision isn't written, it didn't happen. Docs, ADRs, and post-mortems are the real source of truth — and the work.
