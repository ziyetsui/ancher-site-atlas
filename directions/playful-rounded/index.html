How I run the room

Three things I promise every learner.

These aren't rules on a wall — they're how every hangout, course and DM actually works around here.

1

Messy first drafts win.

Nobody learns by lurking. We celebrate the rough version, then make it better together — out loud, on purpose.

2

No gatekeeping, ever.

Every question is a good question. If you feel dumb asking it, that's a sign ten other people are too shy to.

3

Warm beats clever.

Encouragement isn't fluff — it's the thing that keeps people showing up long enough to actually get good.

Before you ask

The questions everyone DMs me.

Do I need any experience to join? +
Nope — most people arrive feeling a little lost and that's exactly the point. Start with the free mini-course, lurk for a week, then jump into a hangout when you're ready.
How much time does it actually take? +
As little as 20 minutes a week. Lessons are bite-sized, hangouts are weekly and optional, and nobody's tracking your streak but you.
Is it just videos, or do real people show up? +
Real people, every week. There's a live hangout, a buzzy chat, study buddies, and me popping in to cheer on your messy first drafts.
What if it's not for me? +
Then you leave with the free starter pack and no hard feelings. The first room's on me — there's nothing to lose by hanging out once.
