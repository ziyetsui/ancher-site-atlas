How I build

Four rules behind every system.

Opinionated on purpose. These are the calls I make so you don't have to.

01

A system you keep beats a perfect one.

If it takes effort to maintain, it dies by Wednesday. I design for the laziest version of you.

02

Fewer fields, fewer decisions.

Every property you add is a tax you pay forever. I cut to the smallest set that still answers your questions.

03

Calm over clever.

No streak shaming, no red overdue badges. The goal is a tool you open without dread, not a guilt machine.

04

Show the ritual, not just the tool.

A template is half the answer. I always pair it with the 20-minute habit that makes it actually run.
