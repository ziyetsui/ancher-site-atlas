How I work

Principles I keep coming back to.

01

Calm beats clever

A feature that explains itself quietly beats one that dazzles and disappears. I design for the second use, not the demo.

02

Show the seams

People trust what they can inspect. When the model is unsure, the interface should say so — not paper over it with confidence it hasn't earned.

03

Reversible by default

Automation should never be a one-way door. Every AI action ships with an obvious undo and a path back to the human.

04

Ship the boring 80%

The magic lives in the unglamorous flows — empty states, errors, the second screen. I'd rather get those right than chase a flashy hero.

Before you reach out

A few honest answers.

What kind of work do you take on?

Mostly AI product surfaces for startups — anything where a model meets a real user and trust is on the line. Full engagements or focused sprints both work.

Do you work fractionally?

Yes. I keep one or two advisory slots open for early teams — usually a day a week, enough to shape direction without slowing you down.

Can you prototype, not just design?

I build working React prototypes when it helps a decision. Real interaction beats a flat mockup for anything AI-driven.

What's your timezone?

Berlin (CET). I overlap comfortably with European mornings and US East Coast afternoons, and I'm flexible for kickoffs.
