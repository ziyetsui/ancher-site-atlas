How I Style

Four Rules · On & Off Set

  1. 01

    One idea, dressed to the teeth.

    A story earns one hook — a colour, a silhouette, a single object. Everything else exists to make that one thing unforgettable.

  2. 02

    The fitting room is the studio.

    The look is decided before the first frame. I over-prepare so the set can stay loose, fast, and a little dangerous.

  3. 03

    Restraint is the luxury.

    Anyone can add. I subtract until only the pieces that have to be there remain — then push one of them too far on purpose.

  4. 04

    Dress the person, not the trend.

    Clothes should make the subject more themselves, not more current. Trends date; presence doesn't.
