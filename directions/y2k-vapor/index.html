03

The Origin Mix

I learned the city by ear — every overpass had a frequency.

Vex started on a cracked controller and a stolen copy of a tracker, looping basement field recordings against four-on-the-floor until 6am. The bedroom became a lab for the after-hours — chrome pads, tape hiss, and a refusal to let a record resolve too soon.

The first residency came from a flyer pinned to a Berlin laundromat wall. The second came from a recording that leaked off a USB stick in Tokyo. Somewhere between the two cities a sound settled: neon-lit, nocturnal, and built to be played loud at the wrong hour.

Today the project is one person, three EPs, and a standing invitation to keep the room moving until the lights come up.
