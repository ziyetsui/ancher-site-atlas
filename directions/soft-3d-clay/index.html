How I work

Three rules I squish everything through.

A tiny studio runs on taste, not headcount — so the principles have to do the heavy lifting.

1

Squishy first

If a character doesn't make you want to poke it, it's not finished. Personality beats polish, every time.

2

Small batches, real love

Editions stay under 500 on purpose. I'd rather sell out a great drop than warehouse a mediocre one.

3

Make the joke land

Every figure carries one small gag. Collectors get it in a glance — that shared wink is the whole point.

Good questions

Before you slide into the inbox.

Do you take custom character commissions?

Yes — one-off vinyl figures and mascot designs for brands and collectors. Lead time is usually 8–12 weeks from sketch to shelf.

Will the sold-out drops ever restock?

Almost never — that's the deal with small editions. Occasionally a tiny artist-proof batch surfaces in the newsletter, so subscribe if you're chasing one.

What's a realistic budget for a collab?

Capsule collabs typically start around the cost of a 500-piece run plus design. Tell me the idea and I'll send a friendly, no-pressure quote.

Do you ship worldwide?

I do. Everything ships padded in recycled, fully recyclable packaging — your buddy arrives un-dented and ready to be poked.
