The Rules

01

Measure or it didn't happen

Every block has a number attached. We test, we log, we compare. Vibes don't make finals — repeatable output does.

02

Hard, but never random

The work is supposed to hurt. It is never supposed to be guesswork. Every hard day is dosed for a reason you can name.

03

Recovery is training

Sleep, fuel and de-load are part of the plan, not a reward for it. The athletes who last are the ones who back off on purpose.

04

One standard for everyone

Olympian or weekend warrior, the bar is honest. I'd rather tell you the truth about your numbers than sell you a comfortable lie.

Straight Answers

Do I have to be an elite athlete?+
No. Half the people I coach have day jobs. The system is the same — pro or amateur, we measure where you are and build from there.
What gear do I need?+
For the app, a phone and access to a gym or open space. For 1:1 we'll scope your setup in the first call and program to what you actually have.
How fast will I see results?+
Speed and power markers move inside 4–6 weeks. Engine work takes a full block. I'll show you the numbers either way — no guessing.
Can I cancel?+
The app cancels any time, no questions. 1:1 runs month to month after the first block. I'd rather you stay because it works.
