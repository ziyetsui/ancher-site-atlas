Before You Ask

Questions People Always Ask.

? Do I need to be "good at art" for the course?

No. You need taste and nerve — both are trainable. We sharpen the eye first, the hand follows.

? Will this style work for a serious brand?

Loud is not the same as unserious. The boldness is dialed to fit the brief — sometimes a whisper, sometimes a shout.

? What's your turnaround for client work?

Posters in about two weeks, full identities in four. I take a few projects at a time so each one gets the noise it deserves.

? Is the course live or self-paced?

Live and cohort-based. Real crits, real deadlines, a real room. Replays exist, but the energy is in showing up.
