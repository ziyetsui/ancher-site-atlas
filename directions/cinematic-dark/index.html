04

How I work

01

Light first, then everything else. If the light is wrong, no lens or grade will save the frame.

02

Hold the shot a beat longer than is comfortable. The truth tends to arrive late.

03

Find the location's own weather before you fight it. The room already knows the mood.

04

Cut to the breath, not the action. A film is paced by what the body does between lines.
