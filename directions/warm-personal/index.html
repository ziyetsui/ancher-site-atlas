Why I do this

I've sat in the seat. I know how lonely steady can feel.

Before coaching, I was VP of People at a 600-person scale-up — hiring through chaos, holding hard conversations, carrying the weight of decisions that touched real lives. I saw brilliant leaders quietly running on empty.

My work isn't about frameworks for their own sake. It's about helping you stay grounded, decide cleanly, and lead like yourself — especially when the ground is moving.

Before you book

A few honest answers.

The questions leaders ask me most often — answered the way I'd answer them on a call.

What actually happens on a chemistry call?+
Thirty unhurried minutes. You tell me what's on your mind; I listen and ask a few real questions. By the end we'll both have a sense of whether we click. No pitch, no pressure, no obligation to continue.
How is coaching different from mentoring or therapy?+
Mentors give advice from their own path; therapy often works with the past. Coaching is forward-looking and yours — I help you think more clearly and act more deliberately about the leadership challenges in front of you right now.
How much time will this take from my week?+
A fortnightly engagement is about one hour every two weeks, plus a little reflection between. Founder Intensives are weekly. I keep the commitment deliberately light — the work is meant to give you energy, not take it.
Do you work with leaders outside your timezone?+
Yes. Sessions are over video, and I keep a couple of early and late slots for clients across timezones. We'll find a rhythm that fits your week, not the other way around.
