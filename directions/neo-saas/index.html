// how I work

Four rules I keep coming back to.

01

Ship the smallest thing that works.

Scope is the enemy. A tool that does one job today beats a platform that ships next quarter.

02

Speed is a feature.

If it feels slow, it is broken. I budget milliseconds the way others budget money.

03

Talk to users before writing code.

The feature you imagine and the one they need are rarely the same. Five conversations save five weeks.

04

Finished beats perfect.

"Done and live" teaches you more than another polish pass ever will. Then iterate in public.
