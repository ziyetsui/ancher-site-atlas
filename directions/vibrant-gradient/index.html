How I think

Before you ask

Questions I get a lot.

Do you work with brands outside DTC?+

Mostly DTC, wellness, and founder-led brands — that's where my playbooks hit hardest. If you're B2B with a real personality, I'm open to it. If you want corporate-safe and beige, I'm probably not your person.

What does a typical engagement look like?+

Most start with a Voice Sprint to lock the foundation, then roll into a Growth Retainer where I run the short-form engine. Founders often add a Ghost track on top. We figure out the right shape on a quick intro call.

Will my team be able to keep this going?+

That's the whole point. Every engagement ships a tone guide, hook library, and a content system documented well enough for an in-house team to run it without me. I'd rather make myself optional than indispensable.

Can you guarantee a follower number?+

No honest person can. What I guarantee is a sharper voice, a repeatable engine, and content people actually want to share. The numbers — like 3 brands past 1M — follow from doing the work right.
