On the work

Statement

I am drawn to the held breath — the instant a place is still, just before it turns into something else.

The pictures are made slowly, on the coast and in quiet rooms, waiting for the light to settle rather than chasing it. What I'm after is the second before change: a kind of stillness that already knows it won't last.

Work has been shown in solo exhibitions in Tokyo (2023) and Berlin (2024), and published in Aperture. Limited print editions are available to collectors.

From a conversation

Tanaka doesn't photograph places. He photographs the moment a place is about to forget itself.

— Aperture, Spring 2024

How I work

Principles

  1. 01

    Wait for the light; never chase it.

    A frame is found, not taken. I will return to a coast for weeks until the air is right.

  2. 02

    Leave the room to breathe.

    Emptiness is composition. What is withheld from a picture carries as much as what is shown.

  3. 03

    Print slow, edition small.

    Each print is made by hand and kept rare, so a single image can still hold a wall.

In the studio now

Now

A new body of work along the Sea of Japan — the same coast, photographed across a single winter.

Most mornings are spent printing the Echizen negatives by hand and editing toward a fourth monograph, due late 2025. A small London show is in early conversation. Prints from the Before the Tide series are available in editions of seven.
