Why I do this

I build interfaces for the moments people don't want to think about. Booking a clinic appointment, splitting a bill, finding the right form at 11pm — the small frictions that quietly tax a day.

I spent six years on healthcare and fintech products where a confusing screen isn't an inconvenience; it's a missed dose or a late payment. That raised the bar permanently. I'd rather ship one screen that works for everyone than ten that demo well.

Maya Chen Product Designer · Toronto
