Frequently asked

Are you available for new projects?

Usually yes, for one project at a time. I book a month or two out and keep a short waitlist when I'm full — a quick message is the fastest way to check my next opening.

How do you price your work?

A fixed project fee for scoped work, or a weekly rate for ongoing and advisory engagements. You'll always get the number in writing before we begin, with no surprise line items later.

How do we get started?

Send a short note with what you're building and your rough timeline. We'll do a 30-minute intro call, and if it's a fit I'll follow up with a written proposal you can say yes to.

What's the working process?

Scope call, written proposal, then weekly check-ins so progress is always visible. At the end you get every file, the source, and a short walkthrough so your team can take it from there.

What timezone are you in?

I'm in CET (UTC+1) and overlap a few live hours a day with teams worldwide, from the US to Asia. Most of the work runs async, so the time difference rarely gets in the way.

What do you actually work on?

Product design and front-end builds for small teams — marketing sites, web apps, and design systems. If your project is near that, reach out; if not, I'm happy to recommend someone.
